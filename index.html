About

Where I'm coming from, and the kind of problems I want to work on.

I'm a computer engineering student at Monufia University building a career in cloud infrastructure and DevOps. My interest started with understanding how systems run reliably at scale, which pulled me toward Linux administration, containerization, and the automation that keeps infrastructure consistent — Docker, Kubernetes, Terraform, and Ansible.

I'm currently a DevOps intern with the Digital Egypt Pioneers Initiative, where I get hands-on with CI/CD pipelines and infrastructure automation, alongside coursework in AWS and Huawei Cloud fundamentals — service models, networking, high availability, and security.

I care about systems that are reproducible and easy to reason about: infrastructure defined as code, deployments that don't depend on memory of manual steps, and monitoring that tells you what's actually happening. I'm looking for an internship or training role where I can apply that while continuing to grow into Azure and Jenkins.
