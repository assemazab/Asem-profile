01 — About

Learning cloud infrastructure by building it

I'm a Computer Engineering student at the Faculty of Engineering, Monufia University (2022–2027), specializing in cloud computing and DevOps. My focus is on turning course theory into practice: standing up CI/CD pipelines, containerizing applications, and automating infrastructure with tools like Docker, Kubernetes, Terraform and Ansible.

I'm currently a DevOps Intern with the Digital Egypt Pioneers Initiative (DEPI), getting hands-on with Linux administration, GitHub Actions, and monitoring stacks such as Prometheus and Grafana. Alongside that, I've completed structured cloud training through the National Telecommunication Institute (NTI) covering AWS, Huawei Cloud, networking architecture, and security fundamentals.

I enjoy problems where infrastructure, automation and reliability meet — the kind of work that makes systems easier to deploy, monitor and trust.
